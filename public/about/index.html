About Me#

Technical writing is more than writing.#

AI can generate a paragraph, summarise a technical concept, and even produce a first draft of a document. I use AI tools in my own workflow, and I see them as an opportunity to work faster and more thoughtfully.

But good documentation starts long before the writing.

It starts with finding the right information, asking the right questions, understanding the product, working with subject-matter experts, identifying gaps, and figuring out what the user actually needs.

That’s what I enjoy about technical writing.

I turn complex technical information into documentation that is clear, accurate, structured, and useful. Whether I’m working on product documentation, API references, user guides, troubleshooting content, or knowledge-base articles, my goal isn’t simply to explain how something works.

It’s to help someone understand it, use it, and get something done.

I also think beyond individual documents. Good documentation should reduce friction, answer real user questions, support product adoption, and build confidence in the product.

AI will continue to change how technical writers research, draft, edit, and maintain content. I’m embracing that change while keeping the parts of technical writing that require human judgement at the centre: context, curiosity, critical thinking, information architecture, and empathy for the user.

That’s the space I work in — where technology, information, and user experience meet..
